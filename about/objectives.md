<p>
  The objectives of this workshop are as follows:
</p>

<ul class="mt-3">
  <li>Provide a forum for scientific, technical, and application‑focused exchange on Agentic AI applied to EO and Earth Science data;</li>
  <li>Present recent advances and emerging approaches in this field;</li>
  <li>Identify key challenges, knowledge gaps, and operational limitations, and outline a roadmap including priority research directions and enabling technologies;</li> 
  <li>Define priority areas for upcoming ESA, international, and commercial initiatives;</li>
  <li>Promote open and interoperable ecosystems, including access to datasets, agent frameworks, benchmark scenarios, communication protocols, and evaluation methodologies;</li> 
  <li>Explore the application of agentic AI in operational and commercial EO products and services;</li> 
  <li>Share practical insights and best practices for implementing agentic systems in practical EO applications;</li>
  <li>Encourage interdisciplinary exchange by drawing on developments in related agentic AI fields;</li> 
  <li>Discuss ongoing and planned scientific activities, pilot projects, demonstrators, and mission concepts incorporating agentic capabilities;</li>
  <li>Engage and inspire early-career researchers and practitioners.</li>
</ul>
