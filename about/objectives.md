<p class="lead">
  The workshop connects researchers, engineers, and practitioners who work at the intersection of
  <strong>agentic artificial intelligence</strong> and <strong>earth observation</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  Our objectives are to share state-of-the-art methods, foster collaboration across academia and industry,
  and identify open challenges where autonomous agents can transform how we acquire, process, and act on
  geospatial and satellite data.
</p>

<ul class="mt-3">
  <li>Provide a forum for scientific, technical, and application‑focused exchange on Agentic AI applied to EO and Earth Science data;</li>
  <li>Review relevant science activities, pilot projects, demonstrators, and mission concepts implementing agentic capabilities;</li> 
  <li>Present the latest advancements in reasoning architectures, multi‑agent coordination, LLM‑based planning, geospatial semantic orchestration, and cognitive onboard systems;</li>
  <li>Identify challenges, knowledge gaps, and limitations in the current state‑of‑the‑art, both technical and operational, and formulate a roadmap with priority research themes, enabling technologies, and promising novel approaches;</li>
 <li>Learn how to implement agentic systems in practical EO applications across multiple domains;</li>
 <li>Define priority areas for upcoming ESA, international, and commercial initiatives;</li>
 <li>Foster open access to datasets, agent frameworks, benchmark scenarios, communication protocols, and evaluation methodologies;</li>
 <li>Investigate the applicability and potential of agentic AI in commercial EO products and services;</li>
 <li>Inspire and support early‑career scientists and researchers by exposing them to cutting‑edge approaches in agentic AI and their relevance to EO and Earth Sciences;</li>
 <li>Draw inspiration from advanced agentic domains beyond EO;</li>
 <li>Bring together experts from AI, geospatial analytics, climate and weather sciences, remote sensing, and space mission operations to address these challenges.</li>
 <li>Unlock a new generation of autonomous, collaborative, and intelligent EO systems—enhancing our capacity to monitor, understand, and respond to Earth system processes while accelerating innovation across scientific, institutional, and commercial domains.</li>
</ul>
